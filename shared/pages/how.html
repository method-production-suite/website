How it's built

Built with AI coding tools, in the open, under rules strict enough for production money.

The Method apps are written by a working producer with AI coding agents, under tests and review. That's said openly here so you can judge the work on its checks, not on a guess about how it was made. The apps themselves contain no generative AI: nothing writes your script or invents your numbers.

Clean room

Every app is independent work. Nobody on the project decompiles, disassembles or debugs another vendor's software, or copies its code, help text, icons, templates or bundled data. Learning how productions work, using other tools normally and reading the open files they export are fine. Each repository's CLEAN-ROOM.md has the full rules, and every contribution follows them.

Money and files

  • Money is whole cents, never floating point. Rates and percentages use an exact decimal type with a written rounding rule.
  • Saves are atomic, with autosave and recovery: a crash never costs you the file.
  • Every file format is plain, documented text, specified in its repository, so your work opens in twenty years with or without these apps.

Supply chain

  • Every dependency must be at least 14 days old before it's used, and every licence and security advisory is checked on every change.
  • Build steps are pinned to exact versions. Released Mac apps are signed and notarized by Apple.
  • MIT OR Apache-2.0, every line on GitHub.
